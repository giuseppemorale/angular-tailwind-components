# Carousel

`TailwindCarousel` presents projected slides one at a time.

Autoplay is optional and **always** pauses on hover and on focus: a carousel that advances on its own and cannot be stopped fails WCAG 2.2.2 (Pause, Stop, Hide). Inactive slides stay in the DOM but carry `hidden` and `inert`, so neither Tab nor screen readers reach them.

## Keyboard

With focus inside the carousel, Left and Right arrows move to the previous and next slide.
